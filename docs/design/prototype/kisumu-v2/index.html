Dawa Fire Drill Kenya · County planning workspace
Facilities, names, locations and costs are invented. Results are illustrative, not validated. About this demo

This is a stress-test sandbox, not a live monitoring system or a government system. Stock, demand, coordinates, facility names and costs come from a reproducible generator (version 5, seed 42), calibrated only to public aggregates. The optimizer (OR-Tools) ranks the fixes; plain-language explanations only restate its result.

A stockout is one facility running out of one commodity within the 90-day run.
